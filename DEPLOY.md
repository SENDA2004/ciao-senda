# Put the site online — 6 steps, about 5 minutes

You do not need to install anything. Everything happens on github.com.

## 1. Create an account
Go to **github.com** → *Sign up*. Free.

## 2. Create the repository
- Click the **+** at the top right → **New repository**
- Repository name: `ciao-senda`
- Choose **Public**
- Click **Create repository**

## 3. Upload the files
On the new empty repository page:
- Click **uploading an existing file**
- Drag in **index.html**, **README.md** and **.nojekyll**
- Scroll down → **Commit changes**

(`.nojekyll` is a hidden file. If your computer won't show it, you can skip it —
the site still works. On Mac press ⌘ + Shift + . to show hidden files.)

## 4. Turn on GitHub Pages
- **Settings** (top of the repository) → **Pages** (left menu)
- Under *Build and deployment* → Source: **Deploy from a branch**
- Branch: **main**, folder: **/ (root)** → **Save**

## 5. Wait one minute
Refresh the Pages screen. It will show:

`Your site is live at https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/ciao-senda/`

## 6. Put it on your phone
Open that link on your phone → share button → **Add to Home Screen**.
It opens like an app.

---

## Important things to know

**Progress is saved per device and per browser.**
Your phone and your computer keep separate progress. To move it:
Progress → Save & Backup → **Export**, then **Import** the file on the other device.

**Speech scoring needs Chrome** (computer or Android). Safari and iPhone cannot listen —
everything else works there.

**To update the site later:** open the repository → click `index.html` → the pencil icon →
paste the new version → Commit. The live site updates in about a minute.

**To keep it private:** GitHub Pages on a free account is public. If you would rather nobody
else sees it, just keep using the file on your computer — it works exactly the same offline.
